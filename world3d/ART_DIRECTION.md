# 3D World — Art Direction (v542, from the user's reference board)

Original world *inspired by* the reference, never a copy of it.

## The look in one line
A moonlit indigo storybook town at night: warm amber life inside every
window, sakura pink against deep purple, violet neon for play spaces,
brass and crown-gold for the brand, stone underfoot, snowcap mountains
on the horizon, festoon lights overhead.

## Palette (locked)
- Night base: deep indigo `#1b1040` → violet shadow `#2a1a5e`
- Stone / plaza: blue-grey stone `#4a4470` with lighter slab lines
- Warm light: amber `#ffb36b`, window gold `#ffd9a0`, brass `#c9a227`
- Brand gold (6IXNETWORKS hall, crown): `#ffd257`
- Sakura: petal pinks `#f9a8d4` / `#f472b6`, trunk umber `#4a2f28`
- Neon play (arcade): violet `#a78bfa`, cyan `#67e8f9`, magenta `#f472b6`
- Foliage: moonlit pine green `#14532d` shaded to `#052e16`
- Water: deep blue `#155e75` with cyan sparkle `#67e8f9`
- Roofs: dark shingle indigo `#171233` with warm ridge trim
- Wood: warm walnut `#6b4226`; fabric awnings: cream, plum, teal

## District identities (same town, different temperaments)
- **Plaza** — the heart: gazebo Wishing Well, flower ring, festoon
  lights, sakura heroes, benches, café terrace spilling out.
- **North row (shopping)** — storefront glass, awnings, icon signage,
  planters; interiors glow through the glass (coffee warm cream,
  clothing bright gallery white-gold, hall grand gold).
- **Arcade (NW)** — neon violet/cyan, playful, darkest glass.
- **Library (NE)** — quiet warm amber, taller windows, book sign.
- **Apartments (SW)** — domestic scale, balconies, soft warm windows.
- **Recreation center (SE)** — bright, sporty green accent, big glass.
- **Residential (W)** — small houses, fences, mailboxes, porch light.
- **Park (E)** — pond, waterfall rocks, stepping stones, sakura grove;
  the calm edge of town (secret garden grows here in a later stage).
- **6IX MUSIC (N backdrop)** — tall block behind the north row, gold.

## Construction rules
- Layered builds: base + trim + roof + sign + dressing. No bare boxes
  for major architecture; pitched/hip roofs, frames around windows
  and doors, awnings over storefronts.
- Materials vary: stone, shingle, wood, glass, metal, fabric — never
  one shiny plastic for everything. Canvas-generated textures for
  asphalt, sidewalk slabs, shingles, awning stripes, signage.
- Repeated dressing is instanced (flowers, lamps, fences, trees).
- Warm point lights are rationed (mobile): well lantern, fountain,
  portal — everything else glows via emissive materials.
- Interiors are staged per building in later versions (v543+); until
  a building's door works, its frontage is glass with a dressed,
  lit interior shell — no fake prompts, no fake buttons.
- Characters must match this direction: warm stylized proportions,
  readable silhouettes at third-person distance (upgrade lands v544).
